# Privacy Policy — Red Alert

*Last updated: [publication date]*

Red Alert is an iOS app that shows disruptions and construction work on the Swiss rail network and warns you when your usual trips are affected. This policy explains which data is processed, why and where. It follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

[First name Last name], [postal address or town], Switzerland — contact: [contact e-mail address]

## Data that stays on your iPhone

The following data is stored **only on your device** and is not sent to the controller:

- your trips (departure station, arrival station, usual time, days, delay threshold);
- your settings (language, map filters, notifications);
- the punctuality history of your trips (60 days at most).

It is deleted when you delete a trip or the app.

## Data sent to the notification server

To send you alerts even when the app is closed, Red Alert sends its server:

- your iPhone's **notification token** (a technical identifier provided by Apple that does not identify you);
- your **language**;
- your **trips** (stations, usual time, days, delay threshold);
- if you follow a trip live, the token of that **Live Activity**.

No name, e-mail address, phone number or GPS location is collected. This data is used only to send your alerts. It is deleted when you uninstall the app (Apple then reports the token as no longer valid) or on request.

The server is hosted by [hosting provider], [country / data-centre region].

## Third-party services

To display information, the app directly queries:

- **opentransportdata.swiss** (open data platform of Swiss public transport): disruptions, construction work and real-time timetables;
- **data.sbb.ch** (SBB open data): official list of stations;
- **transport.opendata.ch**: station search;
- **Apple Push Notification service**: delivery of notifications.

These services receive your device's IP address and the content of the request (for example the stations searched), as with any Internet connection. Red Alert uses no advertising or analytics tools and does no tracking.

## Purchases

Purchases (monthly subscription and lifetime access) are handled entirely by Apple. Red Alert receives neither your name nor your payment details: the app only knows whether Red Alert+ is active.

## Translation

If an official text is not available in your language, it is translated **on your iPhone** by Apple's built-in translator, without sending it to an external service.

## Your rights

You can request access to, correction or deletion of your data at any time by writing to [contact e-mail address]. You can also delete all data by uninstalling the app.

## Disclaimer

Red Alert is not an official app of SBB or any transport company. Information comes from open data sources and may be incomplete or delayed: if in doubt, check sbb.ch or the SBB app.
